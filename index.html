Guest Liability Waiver & Acknowledgment
Tiki
House.
Operated by All-Pro Auto Reconditioning, LLC
This Guest Waiver Form ("Waiver") shall be executed by each Guest prior to entering the Property. By signing below, the Guest acknowledges and agrees to all terms set forth herein as a condition of access.
Defined Terms
Definitions
Approved User
The individual authorized by Ownership for this stay.
Company
All-Pro Auto Reconditioning, LLC, which leases and operates the Property.
Guest
Individual executing this Waiver — visitor in company of Approved User.
Owner
The individual who holds title to the Property, whose identity is known to the Company.
Ownership
Principals and executive leadership of the Company.
Property
The residential property known as the Tiki House, leased and operated by the Company.
Use Policy
Tiki House Use Policy & Agreement, governing document for Approved Users.
Watercraft
Any boat, PWC, kayak, paddleboard, or vessel associated with the Property.
Section 1
Guest Acknowledgment
  • The Guest is accessing the Property solely as a visitor accompanying an Approved User and has no independent right of access.
  • The Guest understands that the Property is a Company-operated asset and that all access is a privilege subject to the Use Policy and this Waiver.
  • The Guest agrees to conduct themselves consistently with the Use Policy and to follow all reasonable directions of the Approved User and Ownership.
Section 2
Property Rules & Care Standards
  • No glitter, confetti, beads, or similar materials anywhere on the Property.
  • No taping, pinning, nailing, or fastening anything to walls, ceilings, doors, furniture, or fixtures.
  • No food shall be left behind in any area of the Property, including the refrigerator, counters, or exterior spaces.
  • No personal belongings shall be left behind upon departure.
  • No furniture shall be rearranged or the layout of the Property altered.
  • No permanent or semi-permanent modifications to the Property are permitted.
  • The Master Bedroom shall not be used unless Ownership has granted written approval for that specific stay.
Section 3
Prohibited Uses
  • Parties or events exceeding 20 persons in total.
  • Commercial activities of any kind.
  • Inviting or admitting individuals not authorized by Ownership.
  • Any activity that creates excessive liability risk, noise complaints after 11PM, or undue exposure of the Property.
Section 4
Smoking Policy
  • Smoking of any kind is strictly prohibited inside the Property — cigarettes, cigars, vapes, e-cigarettes, and cannabis products.
  • Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas. All materials shall be fully extinguished and deposited in proper receptacles.
  • Any evidence of indoor smoking shall result in charges and remediation costs billed through the Approved User.
Section 5
Prohibited Items
  • The Guest shall not leave any contraband, controlled substances, drugs, or drug paraphernalia — whether legal or illegal — on the Property at any time.
  • The Guest shall ensure all such items are removed from the Property upon departure. Violations may result in financial liability and removal from the Property.
Section 6
Watercraft
  • Use of Watercraft is subject to advance written approval by Ownership and is not guaranteed as part of Property access.
  • The Guest shall use all required safety equipment, including life jackets where applicable.
  • All children shall wear life jackets at all times while on Watercraft or docks.
  • No Watercraft shall be operated by the Guest under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any impairing substance.
  • The Company and Owner assume no liability for injuries, accidents, or losses related to Watercraft use. All risk is assumed by the Guest and the Approved User.
Section 7
Financial Responsibility
  • Any damage caused by the Guest to the Property, its contents, any Watercraft, or pool and hot tub equipment may result in financial liability to the Approved User. The Guest agrees to cooperate fully in any damage assessment.
  • Violations of this Waiver may result in the Guest's removal from the Property and the potential loss of future access for the Approved User.
Section 8
Pets
  • Pets are not permitted on the Property unless the Approved User has received prior written approval from Ownership for the specific stay.
  • Approved pets are not permitted on furniture, bedding, or in the Master Bedroom under any circumstances.
  • Any damage caused by a Pet brought by the Guest shall be the financial responsibility of the Approved User.
Section 9
Occupancy & Vehicles
  • The maximum overnight occupancy of the Property is subject to the Use Policy. The Guest shall not admit or invite additional individuals beyond the permitted limit.
  • All vehicles shall be parked in designated areas only. Boats on trailers, RVs, and oversized vehicles require prior written approval from Ownership.
Section 10
Pool & Hot Tub
  • Pool and hot tub use is at the Guest's sole risk. The Company and Owner assume no liability for any injury, accident, or loss arising from use.
  • Pool and hot tub hours are 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Glass containers are prohibited in and around these areas at all times.
  • Children shall be supervised by a responsible adult at all times while in or near the pool or hot tub.
  • The Guest shall not tamper with or add chemicals to the pool or hot tub system.
Section 11
Emergency Protocols
  • In the event of a Property emergency, the Guest shall notify the Approved User immediately so that Ownership can be contacted.
  • The Guest shall not authorize repair services or take actions affecting Property systems without direction from the Approved User or Ownership.
Section 12 — Read Carefully
Assumption of Risk, Release & Indemnification
Express Negligence & Release — Read Carefully
  • The Guest enters and uses the Property — including all indoor and outdoor areas, water access points, docks, recreational features, and any Watercraft — voluntarily and at their own sole risk.
  • The Guest accepts the Property in its current, as-is condition and assumes all risk associated with its features, layout, amenities, and any pre-existing conditions, whether known or unknown.
  • The Company and Owner shall not be liable for any injury, illness, death, loss, or damage sustained by the Guest arising from use of the Property, including claims arising from the Company's own negligence, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
  • The Company and Owner bear no liability for any injury, incident, or damage in which the voluntary consumption of alcohol, cannabis, or any impairing substance by the Guest was a contributing factor.
  • The Guest hereby releases, discharges, and holds harmless the Company, Owner, Ownership, and their respective officers, agents, and representatives from any and all claims, demands, losses, and liabilities of any kind arising from the Guest's presence at or use of the Property.
  • The Guest agrees to indemnify and defend the Company and Owner against any claim, loss, damage, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from the Guest's conduct, negligence, or violation of this Waiver.
Section 13
Severability
If any provision of this Waiver is found to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or severed if modification is not possible. The remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
Section 14
Confidentiality & Nondisclosure
  • The Guest acknowledges that the Property is a private facility and that other individuals present may have a reasonable expectation of privacy. The Guest agrees to respect that expectation in any content shared publicly.
  • The Guest shall not publish, post, or distribute any content — including photographs, video, or written descriptions — that is derogatory, defamatory, or disparaging toward the Company, the Owner, the Approved User, or any other guest or individual present at the Property.
  • The Guest shall not disclose private, sensitive, or personally identifying information about any other individual observed at the Property without that individual's express consent.
  • Any content shared publicly that violates this section may subject the Guest to liability for damages, including reputational harm. The Company reserves the right to demand immediate removal of such content.
